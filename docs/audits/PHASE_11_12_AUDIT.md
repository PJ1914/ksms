# Phase 11 and 12 audit: khata collections, expenses, cash book, daily closing

Audit date: 2026-11-19. Scope: Phase 11 (committed as 2f2faf1) and Phase 12 (the uncommitted finance module, its phone screens and docs).
Method: read every file; ran the existing checks; wrote 9 probes that state a rule and test it on a real database (7 for the cash book and closing, 2 for collections). Each probe was run **before** the fix and failed (7 of 7 and 2 of 2), then passed after it.

## Verdict

Phase 11 (collections) is **sound**: the receipt, ledger, cash book and agent records are written together in one transaction, reversals are append-only, receipt numbers have no gaps under concurrency, and the authorization rules hold. Two rules that were only checked in code could be broken by two requests at the same moment (fixed).

Phase 12 (finance) had **one operational blocker, one money-accuracy defect and a data-loss race**, all fixed:

## Checked and found correct

- **Collections:** the receipt, customer ledger line, cash book line, agent record and audit row are one transaction; a retry with the same key returns the same receipt; five concurrent collections give gap-free receipt numbers and the exact balance; three simultaneous reversals give one success and two `409`; a collector cannot reverse a receipt or credit another agent; collection times in the future or more than 7 days back are refused; impossible start dates answer 422; cross-shop and cross-branch requests answer 404.
- **Finance:** expenses are idempotent and each writes exactly one cash book line; duplicate category names (any case) answer 409; closing the same day three times at once gives one 201 and two 409; opening cash carries from the previous closing; the cashier role is refused every finance route; no secrets in the changes.
- **Money handling:** all amounts are decimal strings and `decimal.js`; no floating-point money in the new phone screens.
