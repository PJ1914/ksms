# Phase 10 audit: GST billing and invoices

Audit date: 2026-11-19. Scope: everything Phase 10 added (tax engine, invoices, invoice numbers, PDF, returns on GST bills, GST settings and screens, tests, documents).
Method: read every changed file; ran the existing checks; wrote an independent check of the tax arithmetic (2,000 generated bills); wrote 9 integration probes that state a rule a GST bill must satisfy and test it on a real database; fixed what was clearly wrong, each fix proved by a probe that failed first.

## Verdict

The **tax arithmetic is correct** and the structure (one pricing code path shared by the cart preview and checkout, invoices written in the same transaction as the sale, immutable snapshots, branch and shop isolation, reconciliation) is sound. The audit found **three high-severity defects in what the customer and the tax office would see**, plus several medium ones; all of those that are clear-cut are fixed. A few items need a decision from the owner or the accountant before GST is used for real bills; they are listed at the end.

## Things checked and found correct

- **Tax arithmetic**: an independent calculation compared with the engine over 2,000 random bills (1 to 6 lines, 7 rates, inclusive and exclusive, discounts per line and per bill, same-state and other-state). Every line's taxable value plus tax equals what is charged; CGST, SGST and IGST add up to the tax; an inclusive price is charged exactly; bill totals are exact sums; the round-off is within 50 paise; discounts are shared to the paisa. No defect.
- **Cart preview and real bill agree** on every tax figure (mixed 5% and 18%, one inclusive and one exclusive item, a bill discount, a buyer in another state).
- **Returns on a GST bill** (7 items at 33.33 with 18% included, returned 3 + 2 + 2) give back exactly the bill total and exactly the bill's tax (35.59); the books still reconcile.
- **Invoice numbering**: 10 bills made at the same instant get numbers 1 to 10 with no gaps or repeats; a cancelled invoice's number is not reused and the invoice is listed as cancelled.
- **Invoice snapshots** of seller, buyer and totals cannot be changed; another shop's or branch's invoices answer "not found"; the PDF file structure (cross-reference table, lengths) is valid.
- **No secrets** in the changes; the new routes are permission-checked and scoped to the shop and branch.

## Decisions needed before GST is used for real bills

| O1  | High     | **Every product that was never set up for GST bills as exempt.** "Taxable" defaults to off, so after GST is switched on, existing products charge no tax and an all-such bill is issued as a Bill of Supply (not valid for taxable goods). Nothing warns the owner                                                                                                                                                                             | When GST is switched on, show how many products have no GST setting and ask to fix them first; or make "not set" a third state that blocks billing |
| O2  | Medium   | **GSTIN is accepted by shape only**: the check digit is not verified, the first two digits are not compared with the state code (a wrong state code sends the tax to the wrong heads), and state codes are not checked against the official list                                                                                                                                                                                               | Add the check-digit rule and the GSTIN-versus-state-code match (needs real, valid GSTINs in the tests)                                             |
| O3  | Medium   | **Place of supply** defaults to the customer's saved state, even for an unregistered customer buying at your counter. For goods handed over at the shop this is normally the shop's own state (CGST + SGST), not IGST                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Accountant to confirm; likely default to the customer's GSTIN state only for registered buyers                                                     |
| O4  | Medium   | **Odd paisa**: CGST and SGST can differ by one paisa (tax 0.05 is split 0.02 + 0.03). Each is meant to be half the rate                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Accountant to confirm the accepted rounding practice                                                                                               |
| O5  | Medium   | The phone's **"Share invoice" shares plain text**, not the PDF the server makes (the app cannot download the PDF yet, as it needs the sign-in token)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Add authenticated download + the phone's share sheet (two small packages)                                                                          |
| O6  | Medium   | **Credit notes**: a return on a GST bill records the tax as one total, not split into CGST, SGST and IGST, and no printable credit-note document exists. Also not enforced: HSN code on invoices (depends on turnover), recipient details on larger B2C bills. **The tax rates offered on the product screen (5, 12, 18, 28) may be out of date**: as far as I know the 12% and 28% slabs were replaced by 5% and 18% in the recent GST reform | Accountant to supply the rate list and the credit-note format; then add                                                                            |
| O7  | Low      | About 15 database calls per bill line (see F6). Fine on a database next to the server (about 50 times faster than here); slow on a distant one                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Batch the stock and price queries before launch                                                                                                    |
| O8  | Low      | Customer and product names in Telugu, Hindi or other scripts print as `?` on the PDF (it uses the built-in plain fonts)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Embed a font that covers them, or print English names only                                                                                         |
| O9  | Low      | Every role that can read invoices also receives the shop's **PAN** in the invoice data                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Hide PAN from roles that do not manage the shop                                                                                                    |

## Questions for the accountant

1. Which GST rates should the product screen offer today?
2. Is "tax rounded per line, then CGST and SGST split with the odd paisa to SGST" acceptable, or must CGST and SGST each be rounded separately?
3. For a customer who is not registered and buys at the counter, is the place of supply always the shop's state?
4. What must a credit note for a return show, and how should it be numbered?
5. Is a plain retail invoice (shop not registered) acceptable in the `B/2026-27/000001` form?
6. Must the HSN code be printed on every line for this shop's turnover?
